DSH Mobile Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

DSH Mobile is made by me, an independent developer. It's a remote client for DeepSeek Harness, an open-source agent runtime that you run on your own computer. Short version of this policy: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your conversations live on your own devices, and I've designed things so that I have no way to read them.

What's stored, and where

Your chats are kept in two places, both yours: a cache inside the app on your phone, and your own Harness server. Nothing conversation-related is sent to me or stored anywhere I control.

The pairing credentials that link your phone to your instance (an instance ID and a device token) are stored in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iOS). They exist so you don't have to re-enter codes every time. They never leave your device except through the encrypted connection to your own server.

About the relay

To reach your computer from outside your home network, traffic passes through a relay server I operate. The relay forwards encrypted bytes between your phone and your computer. It doesn't hold credentials, doesn't decrypt application traffic, and doesn't keep logs of content. If I unplugged it tomorrow, I would have nothing of yours to hand over.

Permissions

The app asks for these, only when you use the matching feature:

  • Camera — used to scan the pairing QR code shown by your computer during setup. No pictures are saved or uploaded.
  • Photo library — used only when you pick an image to send in a chat.
  • Notifications — optional, off by default. Reminders for finished tasks or pending approvals are generated locally on the phone; there is no push server.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment details go to Apple; I never see your card or billing info. What I can see, if anything, is limited to the subscription status Apple reports to the app on your device.

What there isn't

No user accounts. No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No advertising. No cross-app tracking. No server-side profile of you anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete a pairing in the app (Settings, then remove the instance) and its stored credentials are wiped from the phone right away; your computer also revokes that device. Uninstall the app and everything local — chat cache, settings, credentials — goes with it. Conversations on your own Harness server are yours to manage there.

Children

This is a developer tool, not meant for kids. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If something here changes, I'll update this page and bump the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app where practical.

Contact

Questions about any of this: 710790589@qq.com. I read everything sent there.

DSH Mobile 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 25 日

DSH Mobile 由我个人独立开发，是一个远程客户端，用来连接你自己电脑上运行的 DeepSeek Harness（开源项目）。这个政策说白了就一句话：本应用没有账号体系、没有统计埋点、没有广告、没有追踪。你的对话只存在你自己的设备上，我这边没有任何途径看到它们。

什么数据存在哪

你的聊天记录只有两个存放点，都在你手里：手机上应用内的本地缓存，以及你自己的 Harness 服务器。任何对话相关内容都不会发给我，也不会存在我能控制的任何地方。

把你手机和实例绑在一起的配对信息（实例 ID 和设备令牌）存在系统安全存储里（iOS 是 Keychain），目的是让你不用每次都重新输码。除了通往你自己服务器的加密连接，这些信息不会去别的任何地方。

关于中继服务

为了在外面也能连回家里的电脑，流量会经过我自建的一台中继服务器。它只在你手机和电脑之间转发加密字节——不保存凭据、不解密业务内容、不留内容日志。就算明天把它拔了，我手上也没有任何你的东西可以交出去。

权限使用

以下权限只在你用到对应功能时才会请求：

  • 相机——只在配对时扫描电脑屏幕上的二维码用，不会拍照存图或上传；
  • 相册——只在你在聊天里主动选图发送时用到；
  • 通知——可选，默认关闭。任务完成、待审批这类提醒由手机本地产生，没有远程推送服务器。

购买

Pro 高级功能通过 Apple 内购订阅提供。付款信息由 Apple 处理，我看不到你的卡号和账单。我能知道的最多是 Apple 在你设备上报告给应用的订阅状态。

没有的东西

没有账号系统，没有统计分析 SDK，没有广告，没有跨应用追踪，也没有任何针对你的服务端档案。

删除数据

在应用设置里删除某个实例的配对，对应的本地凭据立即清除，电脑端也会同步吊销该设备；卸载应用则本地的一切（聊天缓存、设置、凭据）随之删除。你自己 Harness 服务器上的对话由你在服务器侧自行管理。

未成年人

这是面向开发者的工具，不适合儿童使用，我也不会故意收集 13 岁以下用户的信息。

政策变更

如果这里的内容有变化，我会更新本页面并修改顶部的日期；有实质性变更时会尽量在应用内同步提示。
