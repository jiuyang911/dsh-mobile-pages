DSH Mobile — Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 更新日期：August 2026 / 2026 年 8 月

English: DSH Mobile is provided by an independent developer. This app respects and protects your privacy. This policy explains what information we process and how.

中文：DSH Mobile 由个人开发者提供。本应用尊重并保护用户的隐私，本政策说明我们如何收集、使用和处理相关信息。

1. Where Data Is Stored / 数据存储位置

English: The core design principle of this app is data localization: your conversations, task records and project files always remain on your own device and your own self-hosted Harness server. Neither this app nor its relay service stores, parses, or reads your conversation content.

中文：本应用的核心设计原则是数据本地化：你的对话内容、任务记录和项目文件始终存储在你自己的设备与你自己运行的 Harness 服务器上。本应用及其中继服务不存储、不解析、不读取你的对话内容。

2. Information We Process / 我们收集的信息

English: This app processes the following information only to the extent necessary:

  • Connection information: instance identifier and device pairing token (hashed);
  • Content you submit: chat messages and images you choose to send to your own DSH instance;
  • Subscription information: Apple In-App Purchase subscription status.

中文：本应用仅在必要范围内处理以下信息：

  • 连接信息：实例标识、设备配对令牌（经哈希处理）；
  • 用户提交内容：你选择发送到自己 DSH 实例的聊天消息与图片；
  • 订阅信息：Apple 内购订阅状态。

3. How Information Is Used / 信息的使用

English: We use the information above solely to: provide pairing and remote connection between your device and your own instance; process subscriptions and unlock features; improve product quality and stability; and provide technical support. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or marketing.

中文：我们使用上述信息仅用于：提供设备与你自己实例之间的配对与远程连接服务、处理订阅与解锁功能、改进产品质量与稳定性、提供技术支持。我们不会将你的数据用于广告、分析或营销。

4. Information Sharing / 信息共享

English: We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal information to any third party. Information may only be disclosed when: (a) required by applicable law; or (b) necessary to protect the rights and safety of the app, its users, or the public.

中文：我们不会出售、出租或以任何方式向第三方出售你的个人信息。仅在以下情形下可能披露：法律法规要求；或为保护本应用、用户或公众的权利与安全所必需。

5. Data Security / 数据安全

English: Device tokens are stored hashed and connections are transmitted over encrypted channels. The relay service follows a "zero-persistence" design and does not store any business data. That said, no network transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure — please safeguard your instance credentials.

中文：设备令牌经哈希处理，连接通过加密通道传输。中继服务采用"零落盘"设计，不持久化任何业务数据。尽管如此，任何网络传输都无法保证绝对安全，请妥善保管你的实例凭据。

6. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私

English: This app is intended for professional developers and adult users. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

中文：本应用面向专业开发者与成年用户，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. Policy Updates / 政策更新

English: We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date; material changes may be announced in-app.

中文：我们可能不时更新本政策。更新后将在本页面发布，并更新"更新日期"。重大变更将以应用内提示等方式通知。
