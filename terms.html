What this app is (and isn't)

DSH Mobile is a remote client for DeepSeek Harness, an open-source project released under the MIT license. I'm not affiliated with DeepSeek or any AI provider, and nothing here should be read as their endorsement — those names appear only to describe what the app works with.

The app talks to a Harness server that you install and control on your own machine. Pairing works with an instance code plus a pairing code; there are no user accounts. You're responsible for what runs on your server and what it produces, same as with any software you host yourself.

Subscriptions

"DSH Mobile Pro" is available as auto-renewing subscriptions through Apple In-App Purchase:

  • Monthly plan — 1 month per period;
  • Yearly plan — 12 months per period.

The exact price is shown in your local currency on Apple's confirmation sheet before you pay. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions (App Store). Refunds are handled by Apple under their policies.

You can restore a purchase on any device signed into the same Apple ID via "Restore Purchases" in the app's Pro screen. The free version of the app keeps full single-instance functionality; Pro adds things like multiple paired instances, notification reminders, and conversation export.

Fair use

Don't use the app to break the law or someone else's systems: no unlawful content, no unauthorized access, no leaning on the relay service as a general-purpose proxy. If someone abuses the relay in ways that affect other users, I may cut off its access.

No warranties; limits on liability

The app is provided as-is. AI output is generated by models running on your own setup — verify important results yourself. To the extent the law allows, I'm not liable for losses from model output, downtime of your self-hosted server, or changes to third-party services. Where liability can't be excluded, it's capped at what you actually paid for the subscription in the current period.
